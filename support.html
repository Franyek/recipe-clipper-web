Support

Questions, troubleshooting, and how to reach us.

How does clipping a recipe work?

Share a link — from a recipe website or a YouTube cooking video — to Tine, or paste it in directly. Tine reads the page or video transcript and pulls out the title, ingredients, and steps into a clean recipe card.

How many recipes can I clip?

The Free plan includes 10 clips per month. Pro includes 100 clips per month. Your monthly usage resets automatically and is shown in the app.

Does Tine work with YouTube videos?

Yes — share a YouTube cooking video link and Tine will try to extract the recipe from the video's transcript.

Why couldn't Tine extract a recipe from a link?

Some pages don't contain a clearly structured recipe, or a video may not have ingredients or steps spoken in its transcript. Try a different source, or a page that lists ingredients and steps directly.

How do I sign in?

Tine uses Sign in with Apple — there's no separate Tine password to create or remember.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Pro subscriptions are billed through the App Store. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Tine to manage or cancel.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at the address below and we'll delete your account and associated data. See the Privacy Policy for details on what's stored.
