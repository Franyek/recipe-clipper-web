Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Franciska Sara ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data Tine ("the App") collects, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. We are the data controller for the personal data described below.

Privacy Summary: Your recipes, meal plans, and grocery lists stay on your device. Our servers only store what is needed to run your account, enforce usage limits, and manage your subscription. Page text sent for AI extraction is processed and not stored in any database.

1. Data Stored Only on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and is never uploaded to our servers:

  • Extracted and manually added recipes (titles, ingredients, steps, source links)
  • Recipe images, including photos you pick from your library
  • Meal plans and grocery lists

We do not host a recipe database and do not back up this data. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

2. Data We Store on Our Servers

  • Account data: a unique identifier from Sign in with Apple, and your name and email address only if you choose to share them with Apple’s sign-in prompt (Apple may provide a private relay email instead of your real address). We also store an access token that the App uses to authenticate requests.
  • Usage data: the date and time of each recipe extraction ("clip"), used to enforce the monthly limits of your plan. No URLs or recipe content are stored with it.
  • Subscription data: the transaction identifier, product, purchase period, and expiry date that Apple provides when you subscribe, used to grant Pro access. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Technical logs: our servers keep short-lived technical logs (for example, IP addresses, request times, and error messages) to operate the service, prevent abuse, and fix problems. IP addresses are also used for rate limiting.

3. How Recipe Extraction Works

  • Web pages: when you clip a web page, the page is downloaded and its recipe is extracted from structured data published by the website (such as Schema.org recipe data) or, when that is not available, from the page text using AI.
  • YouTube videos: when you clip a YouTube video, its publicly available transcript is retrieved from YouTube and used for AI extraction.
  • AI processing: for AI extraction, the page text or transcript and its source URL are sent to our server and from there to Anthropic’s Claude API, which returns the structured recipe to your device. We do not store this content in any database; the source URL may appear in our technical logs when an error occurs.
  • Images: recipe images are loaded directly from the original website, which means that website can see your device’s IP address, as with any normal visit.

4. Why We Process Your Data (Legal Bases)

  • To provide the App (account, recipe extraction, usage limits, Pro subscription): necessary to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the service secure (technical logs, rate limiting, abuse prevention, error fixing): our legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To meet legal obligations, where applicable (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

5. Service Providers

We share data only with the providers we need to run the App:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store billing, and subscription verification (Apple Privacy Policy).
  • Anthropic — AI-assisted recipe extraction via the Claude API. Anthropic processes the submitted text to return the result and does not use it to train its models under its commercial terms (Anthropic Privacy Policy).
  • Railway — hosting of our backend servers and database (Railway Privacy Policy).
  • YouTube (Google) — source of video transcripts when you clip a YouTube video (Google Privacy Policy).

6. International Transfers

Some of our providers, including Anthropic and Railway, are based in the United States, so your data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account data and subscription data: for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account in the App (Settings → Delete Account); we then delete this data from our servers within 30 days and revoke the App’s Sign in with Apple access.
  • Clip timestamps: 60 days, then automatically deleted.
  • Technical logs: up to 30 days.
  • Content sent for AI extraction: not stored in our database. Anthropic may retain it for a limited period under its own policy.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its processing, and receive it in a portable format. You can delete your account yourself in the App under Settings → Delete Account. To exercise any of the other rights, or if you cannot access the App, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the EU country where you live. In Hungary, this is the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság (NAIH).

9. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 16 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-Party Links

Extracted recipes contain links back to the original publisher websites. Opening these links takes you to the third-party website, which is governed by its own privacy policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes in the App or on our support page before they take effect, and the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise your rights, contact us at tine+franyek@gmail.com or via our support page.
