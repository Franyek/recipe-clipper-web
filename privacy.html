Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026

[Company/Developer Name] ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how [App Name] ("the App") handles your data and web content.

Privacy Summary: We built this App with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. Your extracted recipes remain on your device, and our servers never retain or log your processed content.

1. Zero Central Storage Architecture

Your privacy is built directly into our application architecture:

  • Local Device Storage: All extracted recipes, ingredient lists, instructions, custom notes, and images are stored exclusively on your individual device.
  • No Cloud Backups of Recipes: We do not host a central recipe database, nor do we store, retain, or save third-party recipe content or URLs on our backend servers.

2. Data Processing & Transmissions

  • Client-Side Parsing: Whenever possible, web content (such as Schema.org JSON-LD metadata) is fetched and processed entirely locally on your mobile device without touching our servers.
  • Transient Server-Assisted Extraction: When a webpage requires server-assisted AI parsing, cleaned, non-identifiable text from the requested page is securely transmitted to our backend server. This data is processed transiently in memory solely to extract the recipe facts and is returned immediately to your device. Our servers do not retain, store, log, or share the submitted text or source URLs once the request is complete.

3. Personal Data & Accounts

We believe in minimal data collection:

  • Account-Free Use: The App does not require user registration or account creation to function.
  • In-App Purchases: Transaction processing for Pro subscriptions is handled directly through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We receive only basic transaction verification tokens without receiving or storing sensitive credit card details.

4. Third-Party Links

Extracted recipes contain direct links back to the original publisher websites. Tapping these links opens the original webpage in a web view or browser, governed by the privacy policy of that third-party website.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
