Privacy Policy — Tine

Last updated August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tine ("we," "our," or "the App") collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account information

When you sign in using Sign in with Apple, we receive and store a unique, anonymized identifier associated with your Apple ID in order to create and manage your account. We do not receive your Apple ID email address unless you choose to share it, and we never receive your Apple ID password.

1.2 Recipe data

When you submit a website or YouTube link to be clipped, we process the content of that page or video (via automated extraction and AI parsing) in order to extract and store the recipe information for your account. This includes:

  • The source URL you submit
  • Extracted recipe content (ingredients, steps, titles, etc.)
  • Timestamps of your clips, used to enforce monthly usage limits on the Free tier and fair use limits on the Pro tier

1.3 Subscription & payment information

Subscription purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your payment card details. We receive limited transaction information from Apple (such as subscription status and renewal dates) needed to grant Pro access to your account.

1.4 Usage data

We may collect basic technical data such as app crash logs and general usage metrics (e.g., number of clips performed) to maintain and improve the App. This data is tied to your account identifier, not to your real-world identity.

2. How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

  • Authenticate your account and sync your data across your devices
  • Extract and store recipe content you request
  • Enforce Free tier (10 clips/month) and Pro tier fair use limits
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for targeted advertising.

3. Third-party services

To provide the App's functionality, we share limited data with the following third parties:

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store, StoreKit) — for authentication and subscription billing.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — the content of pages/videos you submit is sent to Anthropic's API to perform AI-assisted recipe extraction. Anthropic processes this data to return structured recipe information and does not use it to train models on your behalf, per Anthropic's own API terms.
  • YouTube / transcript services — when you clip a YouTube video, we retrieve its publicly available transcript via a third-party transcript API in order to extract the recipe.
  • Railway (hosting) — our backend infrastructure, where account data and clipped recipes are stored, is hosted on Railway's servers.

Each of these providers processes data according to their own privacy and security practices.

4. Data storage & security

Your account data and clipped recipes are stored on our backend infrastructure (currently hosted via Railway) and are associated with your account identifier. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

5. Data retention

We retain your account and recipe data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete your associated personal data and clipped recipes within [X days], except where retention is required for legal or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your rights & choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can:

  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at franyek@gmail.com
  • Request an export of your clipped recipes by contacting us at franyek@gmail.com

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App or via our support page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at:

  • Email: franyek@gmail.com
  • Support page: Tine Support
