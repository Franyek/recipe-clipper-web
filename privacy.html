Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Tine ("we", "our", "the app") lets you save recipes from websites and videos to a clean, personal collection. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique Apple user identifier, and — if you choose to share them — your name and email address. If you use Apple's private email relay, we only ever see the relay address.
  • Recipe links you clip. The URL you submit, and the page or video transcript content needed to extract the recipe, is processed to build your recipe card. Recipe content you save is stored in your account so you can view it later.
  • Usage data. We record how many recipes you've clipped and when, so we can enforce your plan's monthly limit and show you your usage.
  • Subscription information. If you subscribe to Tine Pro, Apple shares subscription status, product, and renewal dates with us so we can grant access. We do not receive or store your payment details — that's handled entirely by Apple.

How we use this information

  • To create and secure your account, and keep you signed in.
  • To extract and save the recipes you clip.
  • To enforce plan limits and show you your usage.
  • To validate and manage your Pro subscription.
  • To respond when you contact us for support.

How recipe content is processed

To turn a link into a recipe card, the page or video transcript text is sent to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) for extraction. This content is used only to generate your recipe card and is not used to train models.

Who we share data with

  • Apple — for Sign in with Apple and to validate App Store subscriptions.
  • Anthropic — to extract recipe content from the links you clip, as described above.
  • Our hosting and database providers — to run the service and store your account and recipe data securely.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Data retention

We keep your account and recipe data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your account record, saved recipes, and usage history, other than what we're required to retain for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

Your choices

  • You can stop sharing your name or email with us at any time by managing your Sign in with Apple settings in your device's Settings app.
  • You can request a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account and all associated data, by emailing us — see below.
  • You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Children's privacy

Tine is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data, can be sent to franyek@gmail.com.
